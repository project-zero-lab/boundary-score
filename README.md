# 🏏 Boundary Score — Cricket Scorer

> An offline cricket scoring app for T20, ODI and Test matches, with full scorecards and PDF export.

**Live:** https://project-zero-lab.github.io/boundary-score/

**Built by:** Chanchal Sharma · Project Zero × Claylab Education Foundation (Cohort 1)

## What it does
- Scores Limited Overs (T20 / ODI / custom overs) and Test matches
- Records runs, wides, no-balls, byes, leg byes, wickets, penalty runs and custom runs
- Tracks batter and bowler stats, partnerships and extras
- Shows run rate, target chase, projected score and a runs-by-over graph
- Handles Super Overs, follow-on, declarations, draws and abandoned matches
- Saves match history and exports the scorecard as PDF or text

## How to use
1. Open the live link
2. Choose **Limited Overs** or **Test Match**
3. Enter team names, overs, players per team and toss result
4. Enter the opening batters and bowler, then tap **Start innings**
5. Select the delivery type, then tap the runs scored
6. For a wicket, tick **Wicket**, tap runs and fill in the dismissal details
7. Use **Undo** to fix a mistake
8. At the end, tap **Download PDF** or **Share PDF scorecard**
